Sector Capital Rotation — informational, not wave-gating

11 Select Sector SPDRs + SPY + GLD, from State Street's own free NAV-history data. Flow is a real creation/redemption signal (share-count change × NAV) — not a price proxy — but it's ETF-vehicle-level, not a complete picture of money entering/leaving the underlying sector; investors can get the same exposure through other ETFs. NAV return is price-only, not total return. The "Inst. 13F (QoQ)" column is institutional-only positioning from SEC 13F filings (all filers, not a curated list) — quarterly and 45+ days lagged, so it confirms whether institutions backed a move, not whether the move is happening sooner.

Insider Activity — informational, not wave-gating

SEC Form 4 open-market insider buys/sells for the BrokerageLink watchlist, filed within 2 business days of the trade. Filtered to transaction codes P/S only (grants, option exercises, gifts excluded) and to non-10b5-1 trades. Cluster buy = 2+ distinct officers/directors buying in the open market in the trailing 30 days — a single buy or sell is never flagged on its own.
